Acme Corp Employee Handbook (Sample document for the RAG demo)

Leave Policy
Full-time employees get 24 days of paid leave per year. Up to 10 unused days can be carried over to the next year. Sick leave is separate: 12 days per year, and a doctor's note is needed for more than 3 days in a row.

Work From Home
Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week with their manager's approval. Core hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST, when everyone must be available online.

Expense Reimbursement
Submit expenses within 30 days through the Finance portal with receipts attached. Travel meals are reimbursed up to INR 1,500 per day. Claims above INR 25,000 need approval from a department head.

IT and Security
Passwords must be changed every 90 days. Laptops must use full-disk encryption. Report lost devices to it-security@acme.example within 2 hours.

Onboarding
New joiners get a laptop on day one, a buddy for the first month, and a 30-60-90 day plan with their manager. Probation lasts 6 months.
